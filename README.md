# 📌 About PerfAI Finance

**PerfAI Finance** is an AI-powered decision support platform designed to help financial institutions and microfinance organizations evaluate credit applications, analyze customer risks, and make faster, smarter, and more transparent lending decisions.

PerfAI Finance is a sector-specific extension of the **PerfAI ecosystem**, adapting its data-driven intelligence approach to address challenges in the financial sector.

The platform does not replace financial experts. Instead, it acts as an intelligent assistant that helps institutions transform customer data into actionable insights.

---

# 🌍 Context

Access to credit remains a major challenge for many individuals and small businesses, especially in emerging markets.

Financial institutions and microfinance organizations often face difficulties when evaluating applicants because of:

- Limited financial history;
- Informal economic activities;
- Lack of reliable customer information;
- Time-consuming manual analysis;
- Difficulty identifying potential risks early.

As a result:

- Some reliable customers may be rejected;
- Some risky applications may be approved;
- Decision-making processes become slower.

PerfAI Finance aims to improve this process by combining data analysis, artificial intelligence, and explainable recommendations.

---

# 🎯 Problem Statement

Financial institutions need to answer a critical question:

> "Can this customer safely receive a credit?"

However, traditional evaluation methods can be limited, especially for customers without extensive banking history.

The challenge is to provide a solution capable of:

- Understanding customer profiles;
- Evaluating credit eligibility;
- Detecting potential risks;
- Explaining recommendations clearly.

---

# 💡 Solution

PerfAI Finance provides an intelligent workflow:


The platform helps financial professionals answer:

> "Is this customer eligible for a credit and why?"

The final decision remains human-controlled, while AI provides meaningful insights.
